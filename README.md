# Multiscale Imaging Datasets for Full-Scale Pore Structure Characterization in Porous Building Materials

**PBM-imaging-datasets** shares representative 2D slices from 3D imaging of ceramic brick and calcium silicate insulation board. The scans were collected as part of doctoral research at KU Leuven to characterize pore structures across imaging scales.

## Browse the images

The ceramic brick XCT collection contains 18 specimen folders across six combinations of specimen size and voxel size, spanning voxel sizes from 0.3 to 4.8 µm. For example, `CB_1.5mm_0.3um_1` identifies the 1.5 mm specimen size, the 0.3 µm voxel size and specimen 1. Acquisition settings accompany some scans in their folders.

## Request the full 3D datasets

The images here are selected slices from the 3D volumes. To request the full datasets, contact **Prof. Hans Janssen** (KU Leuven) at **hans.janssen@kuleuven.be**. Include the material and, where possible, the imaging method, voxel size or specimen folder you are interested in.
